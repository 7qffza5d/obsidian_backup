# Semantic Relations

Let's say we already know what [[Meanings |meanings]] are. This article will be exploring meanings, as in the senses and denotations of words and their relations. I suppose, for clarity's sake, I will define some of the symbols I will use for example sentences. This will stay here until I figure out a better way to put them, I suppose. \* denotes a syntactically or grammatically incorrect sentence, \# denotes a semantically or pragmatically incorrect sentence, and \? denotes a syntactically and semantically incorrect sentence.

Most people know what synonyms are. They are two words with more or less identical meanings. A good test for synonyms are substituting them to see if the meanings change. Let's take *long* and *extended* as examples.
(1) He went overseas for an extended time.
(2) He went overseas for a long time.
(3) I will be seeing my extended family.
(4) \*I will be seeing my long family.
